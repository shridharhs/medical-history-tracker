P.E.S. College of Engineering, Mandya

P.E.S. College of Engineering (PESCE), Mandya, established in 1962 by the People’s Education Society (now People's Education Trust), is one of Karnataka's oldest and most reputed engineering institutions. It is affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, and recognized by AICTE. The college offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs with a focus on academic excellence, innovation, and overall student development. Known for its strong infrastructure, dedicated faculty, and student-centric approach, PESCE continues to shape the future of thousands of aspiring engineers.

PES College of Engineering, Mandya

Role: Full Stack Developer

Passionate about: Digital Health, developing Digital Guest Tracking Systems.

The National Medical Digital Health Record System (NMDHRS) is built with the vision of making healthcare data securely accessible across locations and institutions. In many situations—especially in rural or emergency cases—patients struggle to provide their past medical history, which delays treatment and increases risk.

This project aims to bridge that gap by allowing verified doctors and institutions to upload and view health records in real-time, ensuring that medical history is always available when it's needed the most. It supports both institutional and personal healthcare continuity, improves efficiency, and reduces paperwork.
