What is NMDHRS?

The National Medical Digital Health Record System (NMDHRS) is a government-backed health data platform ensuring secure Aadhaar-linked access to citizen medical records.

  • Encrypted and Aadhaar-authenticated access
  • Only approved hospitals can upload records
  • PDF documents are digitally signed
Rules & Regulations
  1. Only Aadhaar holders can access records.
  2. Data visible only to patient or authorized personnel.
  3. Forging records is punishable under IT Act 66C.
  4. Hospitals must upload records within 24 hrs.
